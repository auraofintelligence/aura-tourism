Questions for tourism and community departments

Tourism brings personal intelligence into public responsibilities for culture, physical activity, environment, services and learning. The invitation concerns developing that capability through a defined contribution and public purpose.

These pages cover Queensland departmental areas, with names shortened for readability. The government directory identifies current responsibilities. The questions are this proposal's suggestions for discussion; participation and authority follow actual decisions. [R23]

Put the larger ambition into a concrete offer

An approach would name the proposed work, responsible delivery organisation and the particular involvement sought. That might concern an XR experience, an agreed information source, operator capability, a venue or evaluation. A department receives enough detail to assess the proposition within its remit.

The wider direction is a domestic programme developing the qualified self and agency in AI-enabled places. Local trials establish a starting experience; later stages build through evidence and willingness to participate.

Questions for service and coordinating departments

Developing personal intelligence also involves public services, planning and accountability. The relevant question is what a proposed connection helps a person understand or do, which organisation answers for it and what information is actually needed. Each department considers a defined role within its remit. [R23]

Connect local development with national ambition

Commonwealth readers across tourism, arts, sport, transport, environment, digital services, employment, skills and social policy would examine the part relevant to them. The proposition is positive capability alongside the rules shaping digital services: people developing their own account, directing AI and engaging thoughtfully with connected places.

A local activity identifies the site owner, operator, council and relevant authority. City data or infrastructure connections have a specific purpose and agreement. Public evaluation uses agreed measures and summaries, while personal records remain within the participant's chosen arrangements.

A clear programme makes those responsibilities reviewable without reducing the ambition to isolated administrative tools. The years before 2032 provide a setting to learn across agencies and communities, with the qualified self giving the work a continuing personal purpose.

Resources for sustained development and useful services

The years remaining before 2032 call for resources that develop people, software and dependable relationships between services. A practical offer separates research and development, the personal Aura arrangement, host services and actual tourism purchases. Costs, rights and support are understandable before anyone commits.

Fund a defined stage of capability

Early work would establish the minimum viable self, chosen observations, reflection and usable continuity. Later stages may add tested sensor connections, personal models, authorised XR experiences and agreed place information. Each stage identifies the work, people, equipment and evidence it is intended to produce.

Let the person retain useful continuity

Some activities may suit existing phones or computers. Other functions require suitable wearables, storage, computing or support. Hardware capability, battery use, connectivity and maintenance affect the experience. An equipment proposal follows the chosen purpose and the actual available technology.

A personal arrangement identifies what the person owns or is licensed to use, what they take elsewhere and what depends on a service provider. A host retains usable descriptions and records if an arrangement ends. Model training and sensitive processing have their own source, cost and access decisions.

Develop a business model people value

Possible models include personal support subscriptions, operator services, development contracts or cooperative arrangements. Each needs clear scope and evidence of benefit. Setup and running charges, referral income, professional work and exit terms are visible. Tourism bookings and event payments retain the actual seller and terms.

A public body might commission a defined demonstration or evaluation where it fits the relevant process. Funding would purchase specified work and reporting, with ownership and reuse terms settled. Grant eligibility and procurement have their own decisions. No established price or revenue outcome is asserted here.

Cultural contributions, access support, volunteer coordination and operators' update time belong in the budget. C-Hours retain their non-monetary reciprocity purpose; financial support and paid work use ordinary financial records. The business case asks whether the capability people gain is worth the real cost of developing and delivering it. Continuing work follows that evidence and the people choosing to support the next stage.
