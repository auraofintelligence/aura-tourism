Selected checks on 28 October 2026

Selected factual and high-change references rechecked against primary publishers. The complete reading retains the author's 27 October 2026 source edition; this register does not claim a fresh review of every source.

View the check notes
  • R1 · The publisher's June quarter 2026 page supports 27.1 million domestic overnight trips and 74.2 million domestic day trips. Counts describe trips, not unique people.
  • R7 · The operator provides date-specific base timetables and directs travellers to its booking and service-status pages for actual availability. This site republishes no departure times or fares.
  • R12 · The ACCC describes consumer guarantees and circumstance-dependent travel remedies. The reading does not promise an automatic refund for every disruption.
  • R13 · The OAIC's current APP overview covers collection, use, disclosure, accountability, integrity, access and correction for covered organisations and agencies.
  • R22 · Queensland Government's organising-committee page identifies Games planning, event coordination and volunteer recruitment as committee responsibilities. No Aura recruitment or partnership is established.
  • R27 · The ACCC-hosted Lonergan Research report is dated February 2025; the 94% smartphone observation belongs to its 2024 fieldwork and 14-and-older population, not a new 2026 survey.
  • R28 · The eSafety hub states that the social media age restrictions took effect on 10 December 2025. This is policy context, not an endorsement of the proposal.

Project sources and domestic tourism

P identifies project material; R identifies public research or guidance. Sources were checked on 27 October 2026. A citation supports a statement; it does not signify endorsement, partnership or approval of Aura. Activity descriptions and pilot quantities are proposals to test.

Current README reviewed 27 October 2026. Working browser tools, editable personal collections and stated limits. The tourism service here is proposed development.

Public legal research intake. Optional questions, tab storage, chosen export and current source checking. Reviewed 27 October 2026.

Working retrieval prototype using held historical Acts. Current acquisition and maintained coverage remain development work. Reviewed 27 October 2026.

Public README. Editable personal memory navigation; illustrative geography and incomplete evidence. An example of form, rather than a completed tourism product.

Project definitions of non-monetary C-Hours, Joyful Responsible Abundance, Try Everything Once and Intermittent Retirement. Reviewed 27 October 2026.

P7 Oceania Healthy De Slop Co ops (opens in a new tab)

Public co-operative and wellbeing proposal. Local arrangements and partnerships are for participating communities to establish. Reviewed 27 October 2026.

R7 North Stradbroke Island ferry timetables (opens in a new tab)

Services privacy volunteering and creative rights

R11 Tourism licences permits and legislation (opens in a new tab)

Business Queensland. Requirements depend on services and location. Its linked commercial activity permit guidance covers protected areas. Confirm the actual activity with the responsible authority.

R12 Travel delays and cancellations (opens in a new tab)

Australian Competition and Consumer Commission. Consumer guarantees, circumstances affecting remedies and distinctions from change of mind or traveller caused missed services.

R13 Australian Privacy Principles (opens in a new tab)

Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. Principles on collection, use, disclosure, accountability, access and correction for covered entities.

R14 Small business and privacy coverage (opens in a new tab)

Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. Explains coverage and exceptions; small size alone does not settle every business’s obligations.

R15 Unpaid work and volunteering (opens in a new tab)

Fair Work Ombudsman. Actual arrangements determine employment status and pay entitlements. A volunteering arrangement may become an employment relationship.

R16 National Standards for Volunteer Involvement (opens in a new tab)

Volunteering Queensland explains Volunteering Australia’s 2024 practice framework. Meaningful roles, recruitment, support, safety, recognition and improvement.

R17 Not for profit organisations and volunteers (opens in a new tab)

WorkSafe Queensland. Duties depend on the organisation and activity; volunteer workers’ compensation coverage requires particular care.

R18 First Nations cultural and intellectual property protocols (opens in a new tab)

Creative Australia. Guidance for cultural collaboration, self-determination and appropriate use. Relevant people determine cultural permissions; copyright and contractual questions also arise.

R19 Music licensing for events and festivals (opens in a new tab)

OneMusic Australia. Public performance licensing and relevant rights. Performer payment and permission to film or reuse material are separate questions.

R25 Queensland road conditions and traffic (opens in a new tab)

R26 Beachsafe (opens in a new tab)

Connected devices intelligence and public policy

R27 Smartphone use in the ACCC consumer survey (opens in a new tab)

Lonergan Research, February 2025, for the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission. Page 37: 94% of respondents aged 14 and older had a smartphone for personal use. Fieldwork October-November 2024; sample 3,075.

R28 Australian social media age restrictions (opens in a new tab)

eSafety Commissioner. World-first minimum-age requirements in effect from 10 December 2025, with current implementation and guidance. Policy context, not endorsement of Aura.

R29 Phone sensors and their observations (opens in a new tab)

Android Developers. Motion, position and environmental sensor categories. Available hardware and permissions depend on the actual device.

R30 International AI Safety Report 2026 (opens in a new tab)

February 2026 scientific synthesis of general-purpose AI capabilities and risks. Context for increasing capability and uncertainty, not a prediction of SI arrival or evidence of Aura performance.

R31 AI with glasses and extended reality (opens in a new tab)

Google, I/O 2025. Demonstrated direction of AI using camera and microphone context through XR devices. Product availability and any Aura implementation require separate verification.

R32 Queensland smart pedestrian crossings (opens in a new tab)

Department of Transport and Main Roads. Sensor responsive timing installed at Queensland sites, including Brisbane. An existing infrastructure example, not an Aura integration.

R33 Cleveland Point asset monitoring sensors (opens in a new tab)

Redland City Council. Trial for public asset use and maintenance. Council identifies the purpose and limited access; a public visitor data feed is not assumed.

R34 Wearable heart rate observations (opens in a new tab)

Apple Support. Explains heart rate measurement and factors affecting readings. An observation is distinct from an explanation of personal experience or a clinical assessment.
