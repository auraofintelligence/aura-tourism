Site map

Every page, at your pace

Start anywhere, follow a chapter sequence or look up a source. The original reading remains available without JavaScript.

01

Home

02

Meet your Aura

Begin with a person, their own words and what they want from an experience. Explore the qualified self, a minimum viable starting account and an Aura that returns its interpretations for correction.

03

Two travel stories

Follow two imagined visits to Straddie: a person travelling alone and a group with different interests. Their choices, access needs and reflections shape the encounter, and what comes next.

04

Reflection and memory

A measurement cannot explain a lived experience on its own. See how personal explanations, reflection, correction and a memory palace could connect one encounter with the next.

05

Music, sport, culture and nature

Find your scene through music, have a go at sport, meet the people who host a place, or enjoy nature at your own pace. The proposal treats pleasure and participation as settings for personal development.

06

Access and participation

Useful access information is specific to the person and the actual environment. Explore how chosen personal context and a conversation with the host could support participation and self-development.

07

Hosts, volunteers and C-Hours

Tourism operators, volunteers and community hosts each bring something different. Follow the proposed relationship before, during and after a visit, and how voluntary contribution could receive non-monetary recognition.

08

Services, rights and data

Connect a person’s intention with actual service responsibilities. Read the proposal’s approach to relevant rules, clear offers, changes, personal data ownership and the difference between sensing, interpretation and action.

09

Straddie, Redlands and Brisbane

Begin with a bounded local experience on Straddie, then explore a dependable second setting through Redlands and Brisbane. Personal context and changing place conditions need identifiable sources and responsibilities.

10

Australia and Brisbane 2032

The proposal uses Brisbane 2032 as a horizon for growing useful personal and civic capability. Its staged sequence is an ambition to test, with Games roles and recruitment remaining the responsibility of the organisers.

11

Public agencies and resources

Explore specific questions for tourism bodies, service departments and coordinating agencies, followed by the people, equipment and continuity a defined stage would need. These are proposed discussion questions, rather than agreed partnerships or funding.

12

Evaluation and invitation

Review whether the self-account is faithful, whether correction helps and whether people gain practical agency, enjoyment and continuity. The invitation leaves room for learning, contribution and chosen rest.
