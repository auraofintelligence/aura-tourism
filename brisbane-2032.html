The original reading · Luke Nathan Hayes · 27 October 2026

An Australian opportunity in personal intelligence

Australia has a substantial domestic setting in which to develop this relationship. Tourism Research Australia reports 27.1 million overnight trips and 74.2 million day trips by Australian residents in the June quarter of 2026. Those are trips, not unique people. Repeated domestic experiences offer practical opportunities to examine a personal Aura growing through different places and stages of life. [R1]

Shape digital life through positive capability

Australia's social media minimum-age legislation is a world-first national step. Since 10 December 2025, covered platforms have been required to take reasonable steps to prevent under-16s creating or keeping accounts. The eSafety Commissioner, Australia's online safety regulator, explains the obligations and implementation. [R28]

My argument is that Australia has a reason to extend its digital ambition into positive ways of living with AI, SI and XR. Aura is my proposed approach: develop the qualified self, place useful intelligence in a personal relationship and let people learn through worthwhile real-world experiences. Legislation and practical invention contribute different parts of that national direction.

Connect tourism leadership with a defined programme

Tourism and Events Queensland, or TEQ, is the State's lead marketing, experience development and major events agency. Destination 2045 is the Queensland Government's long term tourism plan. A proposal to them would connect domestic visitor experiences with personal intelligence, creative participation and the evidence from local development. [R2, R4]

Tourism Australia is the Australian Government tourism marketing agency. Its statutory objectives include domestic travel, while its current public role description emphasises international visitor attraction. Australian trials would first establish usefulness here, with later industry learning considered within its remit. [R3]

Austrade coordinates THRIVE 2030, the national visitor economy strategy. Its 2026 action plan addresses regional experiences, modernisation, workforce, accessibility, sustainability and better data. A qualified-self programme would bring a specific proposition to those priorities: develop people's understanding and capability through the experience of visiting and hosting a place. [R5]

Offer a domestic path with wider relevance

The initial geography is Straddie, Redlands and Brisbane, then other willing Queensland and Australian settings. Each has its own hosts, cultural relationships, infrastructure and capacity. The aim is a transferable personal relationship, with local differences understood rather than erased.

The years before 2032 offer time to create evidence, build useful capability and develop commercial and public arrangements. A successful demonstration would show what people and their Auras have learned through living, supported by dependable services and communities that find participation worthwhile.

Developing towards Brisbane 2032

From October 2026, Australia has less than six years before Brisbane's Olympic and Paralympic Games. The window calls for an early start and sustained development, with people, personal Auras and host communities learning together. The Games provide a public milestone; useful progress begins through ordinary experiences well before it.

A proposed sequence for the years ahead

The stages below describe my proposed development direction. Timing would respond to results, resources, available technology and the people choosing to participate. It is not an official Games programme.

Build continuity between stages

A starting participant might return through several experiences, retaining useful records and choosing what develops next. Their Aura would be tested for faithful recall, correction, appropriate initiative and handling of new contexts. Hosts would examine the visitor experience and practical effort, including what breaks when a function moves to another setting.

Technology would be reviewed as it becomes available. A new phone, wearable, model or XR interface enters through a defined use and suitable testing. The personal relationship and usable records give continuity as components change. More capable intelligence is an opportunity to examine through results, rather than a date the programme needs to predict.

Develop people as well as software

Participants would learn to question explanations, recognise uncertainty, direct an assistant and understand the effects of sharing information or authorising actions. Volunteers and hosts would develop practical judgement through real roles. Creative and cultural experiences give that learning enjoyment and meaning.

The wider aim is Australians growing into capable participants in an increasingly intelligent environment. That includes people creating experiences, improving services, contributing to community decisions and choosing how to spend their time. The qualified self remains the organising purpose through that development.

Public evidence would identify what has been demonstrated, what remains experimental and the resources needed for continuation. By 2032, a substantial working example would have years of experience behind it. The continuing value after the Games would be people, relationships and capability that remain useful in everyday life.

Civic capability and the Brisbane 2032 horizon

Brisbane 2032 is Queensland's Olympic and Paralympic Games. The Brisbane Organising Committee for the 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games manages delivery, including volunteer recruitment, marketing and Games-time venue operations. Official roles follow its processes. [R22]

Aura's proposed civic pathway uses the years beforehand to develop people and their personal intelligence relationships through real experiences. Tourism, music, sport, culture and environmental activity offer settings for learning judgement, communication, practical skills and care for a place.

Grow capability through experience

A person might begin by welcoming visitors at a small event. Their Aura would help explore the role beforehand, find approved information and reflect afterwards. Later opportunities might involve more responsibility or a different kind of work, with competence assessed for the actual role.

An authorised XR rehearsal might make a venue or scenario more understandable. Translation or contextual assistance may support a conversation. Those functions would be tested for their actual purpose, with the person learning when to rely on them, ask a human or question an interpretation.

The qualified self retains more than an attendance history. It develops a person's understanding of what they enjoy, how they respond to unfamiliar situations and what they want to contribute. Their Aura would connect reviewed experience with future learning and invitations, under chosen information arrangements.

Bring a demonstrated proposition to organisers

For the organising team, a future proposal would show working examples and evidence from earlier settings. It would identify the particular role, technology, support, privacy, cost and benefit. A personal Aura might assist an approved task, while official selection, training, accreditation and deployment retain their actual responsibilities.

Any integration or public partnership description would follow an agreement. An Aura account or contribution record does not confer an official Games role. Local civic development remains worthwhile for people who never apply, including those pursuing paid work, creative projects or community participation elsewhere.

Make 2032 an occasion to demonstrate a lived result

The ambition is for Australians to arrive at that horizon with years of practical experience in directing intelligence and engaging with connected places. A visitor sees useful capability in action; a volunteer understands their role; a host has a dependable service; the person retains a developing account of their own life.

The result is judged through demonstrated usefulness and the experience of communities hosting it. The same relationships then continue beyond an event, giving the wider AI and SI transition a personal, social and practical foundation.
