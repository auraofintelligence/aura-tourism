The original reading · Luke Nathan Hayes · 27 October 2026

Testing the qualified self and practical agency

The central question is whether people develop a more useful understanding of themselves and a more capable relationship with their Aura through experience. A satisfactory booking or enjoyable outing is part of that evidence. The larger test concerns meaning, judgement, correction, learning and practical agency across time.

Examine what the person recognises and uses

The first review checks whether the minimum viable self is recognisable and its sources understandable. Later encounters examine whether the Aura uses context faithfully, responds to corrections and keeps uncertainty visible. The person is able to explain why an interpretation fits or misses their experience.

Test connections through actual use

Sensor work checks the device, conditions, data quality and the meaning assigned to observations. XR work examines understanding, comfort and accurate presentation of authorised material. A place connection is tested against its actual source, access and update process. A function working in one setting is examined again when circumstances change.

Participants would also learn how to question the AI and direct actions within agreed boundaries. Relevant comparisons include the current personal record, the usual service process and outcomes at later visits. A small voluntary trial has limits, which remain visible when discussing wider benefit.

Review benefit burden and unwanted effects together

Visitors, hosts, volunteers and residents contribute to the account. The review includes time, costs, errors, misunderstandings, information concerns and reasons for stopping. A serious issue prompts an appropriate response and a pause in the affected function where needed, with responsibility for resuming identified.

Formal human research would assess ethics review and other applicable requirements before recruitment. A medical function would follow a separate clinical evidence and regulatory process. This tourism proposal concerns personal development and experience; sensor observations do not establish treatment benefit.

The next stage identifies demonstrated benefit, remaining questions and the work needed to resolve them. Public reporting gives participants and agencies a reasoned basis for continuation. By 2032, the intended result is evidence of qualified selves and personal intelligence relationships developing through life, including their limits and the resources needed to sustain them.

Joyful Responsible Abundance through personal intelligence

Joyful Responsible Abundance gives Aura's development a direction. Joyful concerns enjoyment, curiosity, creativity, relationships and rest. Responsible asks about consequences for people and the environment, including who carries the costs. Abundance concerns wider access to useful knowledge, resources, opportunity and practical freedom. [P6]

Let greater capability enrich a whole life

The qualified self is central because a satisfying life is understood through the person living it. A visitor may discover music they want to make, a skill they want to learn or a way of spending time that gives them more room to engage. Their Aura would connect those discoveries with earlier context and chosen next steps.

Body observations, memories, reflection and the environment each contribute a different kind of understanding. XR offers ways to explore and share selected experiences. Increasingly capable AI offers new possibilities for explanation, creation and appropriate assistance. The person develops judgement through working with that capability and questioning it.

Make the national ambition personal and social

My proposal is that Australia uses the years before 2032 to demonstrate a positive relationship with this technological transition. Tourism provides an enjoyable domestic setting in which people, hosts and communities learn together. Music, sport, culture and environmental activity give the work depth beyond a technology demonstration.

Preparing for SI means developing useful personal context and ways to direct intelligence now, while assessing new capability as it emerges. The programme does not depend on predicting an arrival date. It develops a continuing relationship that is able to be examined and revised as the world changes. [P1, R30]

Keep room for learning contribution and rest

Intermittent Retirement is Aura's wider proposal for funded periods of rest, care, learning and travel at different stages of life. Its financial and time arrangements would be established for an actual opportunity. Tourism offers one possible setting for that exploration. A holiday is also worthwhile in itself. [P6]

The developing Aura has room for abilities and ambitions as well as ordinary pleasure. A person may choose civic contribution, creative work, new friendships or a quiet afternoon. Growing capability is valuable through what it adds to those choices and the consequences it helps people understand.

A Protopian gambit means pursuing improvements that are tested and revised through experience. Here that begins with a person recognising themselves in their Aura and finding something worthwhile to do together. The larger future develops through those encounters, with Joyful Responsible Abundance judged through life as it is actually lived.

A practical invitation to develop the first experience

The proposal is to develop Aura as an Australian way of growing the qualified self alongside increasingly capable intelligence and connected environments. Domestic tourism offers a practical beginning on Straddie, with a path through Redlands, Brisbane, Queensland and Australia. The years to 2032 give that relationship time to mature through real experience.

For visitors and residents

A first encounter would show the purpose, working tools and proposed next development. The person chooses an experience worth exploring and the context they want to bring. Onboarding records a minimum viable self, followed by an agreed activity, selected observations and a review of their meaning.

The person examines what their Aura has understood and changes what it has missed. Later encounters test whether those corrections help. Equipment and information use follow the actual arrangement, with a usable copy and a clear way to pause or leave. The experience is an invitation to learn with intelligence through living.

For operators volunteers and community hosts

A host might bring a lesson, performance, cultural experience, recreational activity or community role to develop. The proposal identifies the experience, authorised material, relevant observations and the practical work required. Cultural hosts and land managers determine the participation and permissions relevant to their setting.

Operators and volunteers would learn through the work as well. An approved XR rehearsal, a clearer explanation or thoughtful guest feedback might improve an actual service. Agreements identify responsibilities, information handling, costs, support and what happens when arrangements end. Published roles follow what people have actually agreed to do.

For tourism bodies and public agencies

The invitation is to consider a staged programme of personal and civic capability, with a concrete first experience to examine. A request would name the work, delivery organisation, resources, evidence and particular role sought. Public systems and Games connections follow the relevant process and actual agreements.

Australia has already shown a willingness to shape digital life through social media legislation. This proposal asks it to pursue an equally purposeful positive direction through AI, SI and XR. Aura is my proposed route: people developing the context and judgement to work with intelligence, while communities shape how their places participate.

The immediate result to build is a person who recognises their starting self, enjoys a meaningful encounter and understands more about what it taught them. Repeated through the years before 2032, that relationship has room to develop into adaptive whole of life support, with useful capability continuing long after a single visit or event.
